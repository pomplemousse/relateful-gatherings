Questions, answered honestly

How do I pay?

Payment happens when you grab your ticket through the link above — pick whichever tier feels right, $10, $15, or $20. Tickets are purchased in advance; we can't guarantee a spot for anyone who shows up without one.

Is this therapy?

No. This is a practice space, not therapy — our method is educational, not diagnosis or treatment. The facilitator is a guide, not a therapist, and won't prescribe choices or actions. We just stay present together, trusting you're already whole rather than broken and needing fixing. You're responsible for your own feelings and safety here.

Is it okay to feel angry, bored, annoyed, or shut down here?

Yes. Feelings people call "negative" — anger, boredom, frustration — are part of you too, and this practice makes room for them. We invite you to notice them as they arise; that awareness is what gives you a choice instead of being driven by them unconsciously. It's okay to ask for help if it gets hard to navigate. There's a difference between feeling something and acting on it without awareness — that's where you start to leave the practice.

How do I know if I'm doing Relateful correctly?

There's no perfect way to do it, but a good check-in: are you connected to what's happening for you right now? Where's your attention actually at? It's easy to slide into storytelling, analyzing, or giving advice — all useful in their place, but we invite you back to your immediate experience: sensations, emotions, impulses, whatever's actually arising. Presence comes less from talking about your experience and more from staying with it as it happens.

How do I know if it's right for me?

Our practices can bring up a lot, and you're the best judge of your own readiness. If this sounds like it could be overwhelming or destabilizing for you, if you're managing mental illness, addiction recovery, or significant emotional challenges that this kind of environment could stir up, or if you're not confident you can take care of your own needs while you're here, we'd advise sitting this one out. You'll also need to be at least 18 to attend.

Is the facilitator responsible for my safety?

This is a brave space, not a safe space — the facilitator isn't responsible for your safety, you are. We treat you as a free agent, capable of honest self-assessment and making your own choices. That means taking responsibility for yourself and your experience, including setting and holding your own boundaries. By attending, you're choosing to look after your own safety and comfort — and you can always step back or leave the practice in any given moment.

Is there a code of conduct?

To keep this a relaxed, comfortable space for everyone, we ask that you hold a few simple agreements:

  • We start and end on time.
  • This space holds room for disagreement — you're free to explore different opinions, attitudes, and ways of being, as long as you stay respectful.
  • If the facilitator feels someone's behavior is disrupting the space, they may be asked to step out for a session or more.
  • Zero tolerance for violence, threats, or intimidation — this means immediate removal from the space.
  • Ask before you touch someone, every time. Silence or hesitation is a no, not a maybe.
Do I need experience?

None. This is built for people who are completely new to this kind of practice.

What if I don't want to share?

You're never forced to share. You can always pass.

Why only 12 people?

To protect the quality of presence and practice — small enough that real awareness can actually happen.

What should I bring or wear?

Just show up comfortable — see "Before you come" in the timeline above (water, a cushion or mat). We provide chairs too if you'd rather sit up.

Is this spiritual or religious?

No particular belief system required — just a willingness to be present and honest.
