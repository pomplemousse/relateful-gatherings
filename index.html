what we actually do

We call it a flow.

We gather in a small circle — 6 to 12 people. I open with an invitation. From there, we drop into our present-moment experience: sensations, feelings, states — and share from right there, not from the story in our heads.

🌱 First, you get deeply connected to yourself.

🤝 Then, gradually, you become aware of each other.

🌀 Eventually, aware of the whole group — expanding your capacity to be aware, together.
